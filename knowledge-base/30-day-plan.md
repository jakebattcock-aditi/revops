# 30-Day Plan (starts Wed Oct 7, 2026)

Goal: first paying Upwork client within 30 days, on a budget, without putting the Quadient job at risk. Nothing here is guaranteed. The plan is built to make results measurable so you can adjust fast.

## Rules

1. **Quadient is the base.** Freelance work happens only outside work hours, on personal devices and accounts. See the confidentiality section in `case-studies.md`.
2. **Connects budget.** About $0.15 per Connect. Cap the first week at about $25–30 and decide again after the numbers come in.
3. **45–60 minutes a day, not more.** Sustainable beats heroic.

## Daily routine (45–60 min, evenings or lunch on a personal device)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Run the job search (ask Claude: "find jobs"); pick 2–3 that score 4+ |
| 15 min each | Build the giveaway, record Loom, send application |
| 5 min | Check messages. Reply within minutes if someone responds |

## Targets

| Week | Applications | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10–12 | Cost per application, view rate |
| 2 | 12–15 | View rate and replies; adjust first line and rate |
| 3–4 | 15–20 | Double down on the job types that get views |

**Decision rule:** after about 15 applications, if fewer than 1 in 7 are viewed, change the profile and first lines before spending more Connects.

## Rate

Your profile rate is $75/hr. With no reviews, consider applying to the first 2–3 jobs at about $55–65/hr or as a small fixed-price starter project (such as a CRM audit). The first review is worth more than the rate difference. Raise rates after 3 five-star reviews.

## Free channels (no Connects)

- **Invitations** from clients cost no Connects to answer. Keep availability on and the profile sharp.
- **LinkedIn:** message 5 RevOps/sales leaders at small companies per week with a short, specific audit offer.
- **Warm network:** AltaML and Showpass contacts, friends running small businesses. Offer a small paid audit.
