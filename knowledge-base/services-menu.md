# Services Menu

Rates are starting ranges in USD. Adjust per scope, client size, and Upwork competition.

## 1. Fractional RevOps

**Pitch:** Your part-time RevOps lead — I own the CRM, the tool stack, and the forecast so your sales team can just sell.

**Rate:** $3,500–$7,000/month retainer (10–20 hrs/week), or $75–$110/hr ongoing.

**Fits:** Ongoing Salesforce admin, Gong/Clari administration, reporting/dashboards, quota and forecast setup, "we need someone who owns this."

## 2. RevOps Consulting

**Pitch:** Fixed-scope diagnosis and decision support — audits, tool evaluations, and clean data you can actually trust.

**Rate:** $90–$140/hr, or $1,500–$6,000 per fixed-scope engagement.

**Fits:** CRM/field-mapping audits, Gong vs. Clari (or any vendor) evaluations, license modeling, role hierarchy redesign, data privacy/DPIA support for sales tools.

## 3. RevOps Automation Build

**Pitch:** I build the integrations and automations that move your revenue data between systems — without the silent failures.

**Rate:** $2,500–$15,000 per project (fixed price), or $95–$150/hr.

**Fits:** Zapier/multi-org Salesforce syncs, JavaScript code steps, partner/compensation data feeds, workflow migrations, broken-automation rescue.
